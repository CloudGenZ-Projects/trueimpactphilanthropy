Fractional Leadership

What is a fractional fundraising director, and when does a nonprofit need one?

A fractional fundraising director provides senior fundraising leadership on a part-time or contracted basis. The organization gains strategic expertise without immediately carrying the cost and commitment of a full-time executive hire.

This can work well when an Executive Director is carrying too much fundraising responsibility, when junior staff need senior guidance, during a leadership transition, or when an organization is growing but not yet ready for a full development director.

What nonprofit leaders should consider

The role should not simply become an outsourced task list. Strong fractional leadership helps set revenue priorities, review pipelines, guide donor and partnership strategy, coach staff, engage the board and create accountability around implementation.

Putting it into practice

For some organizations, the best outcome is not to keep a fractional consultant forever. It is to strengthen the fundraising function enough that the organization eventually knows what permanent structure it actually needs.
