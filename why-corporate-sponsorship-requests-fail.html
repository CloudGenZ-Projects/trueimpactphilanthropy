Corporate Partnerships

Why most nonprofit sponsorship requests go nowhere.

Many sponsorship requests begin with what the nonprofit needs: a dollar amount, an event and a list of logo benefits. The company is left to figure out why the opportunity matters to them.

A stronger approach starts with alignment. What does the company care about? Which communities, employees, customers or ESG priorities intersect with your mission? Is there a natural relationship to build, or are you simply looking for a company with money?

What nonprofit leaders should consider

Then think beyond sponsorship. A strong corporate relationship might include employee engagement, expertise, in-kind support, introductions, visibility, workplace giving or multi-year investment. The right partnership can create more value than a one-time cheque.

Putting it into practice

Fewer, better-researched prospects often outperform a large list of generic outreach. The objective is not to send more packages. It is to create more relevant conversations.
