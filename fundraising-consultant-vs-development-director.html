Nonprofit Leadership

Should your nonprofit hire a fundraising consultant or a Development Director?

A permanent fundraising hire makes sense when the organization has enough ongoing work, leadership support, budget, systems and strategic clarity to sustain the role. A consultant can make more sense when the organization first needs diagnosis, strategy, specialized expertise or temporary senior leadership.

Hiring someone into a weak system does not automatically fix the system. If goals are unclear, the board is disengaged, the CRM is unreliable, the case for support is weak and revenue priorities are scattered, a new employee may inherit the same problems.

What should nonprofit leaders consider?

Consulting can help leadership decide what should be built before adding headcount. Fractional leadership can bridge a gap while the organization develops capacity or recruits. A permanent hire can then enter a clearer role with stronger expectations and infrastructure.

What this means in practice

The right answer depends less on whether consultants or employees are “better” and more on what problem the organization is actually trying to solve.
