Fundraising Consultant vs. Development Director: Which Do You Need?

A permanent fundraising hire makes sense when the organization has enough ongoing work, leadership support, budget, systems and strategic clarity to sustain the role. A consultant can make more sense when the organization first needs diagnosis, strategy, specialized expertise or temporary senior leadership.

Join the conversation

Not a publication. A conversation.

Let’s Talk Philanthropy is designed to become a place where nonprofit leaders, fundraisers, board members, donors and community organizations can respond to what they read and add their own perspective.

Read what’s being said.

Articles will explore the practical and sometimes uncomfortable realities of fundraising, power, generosity, impact, organizational growth and the nonprofit sector.

Say what you think.

Each article can include a moderated comment area so readers can share experiences, disagree respectfully, ask questions and contribute lessons from their own organizations.

Bring the sector into the room.

The strongest conversations do not come from one consultant having all the answers. They come from people doing the work sharing what they are seeing, learning and questioning.
