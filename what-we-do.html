Services

Fundraising support built around what is actually holding your organization back.

You may need a fundraising strategy. You may need stronger partnerships, better systems or senior leadership. Sometimes you need several of these working together.

01

Fundraising Strategy & Planning

We assess your current fundraising, identify realistic revenue opportunities and create a focused plan for growth.

Fundraising audits
Annual fundraising strategies
Revenue diversification
Donor pipeline development
Major gifts and stewardship strategy
Fundraising systems and processes

Best for: Organizations that know they need to raise more but are unsure where to focus, or preparing for growth.

02

Corporate Partnerships & Sponsorship

We help you move beyond cold sponsorship requests and build partnerships aligned with both your mission and the priorities of potential partners.

Corporate partnership strategy
Prospect identification
Sponsorship packages and proposals
Relationship development
Employee-giving opportunities
Partnership stewardship

Best for: Organizations that want to move beyond cold sponsorship requests and build long-term, high-value corporate relationships.

03

Grants & Institutional Funding

Grant writing is most effective when it is part of a clear funding strategy. We help organizations become grant-ready and make smarter funding decisions.

Funding research
Grant strategy and calendars
Proposal development
Case for support development
Grant readiness

Best for: Organizations looking to become grant-ready, build structured funding calendars, and pursue high-probability institutional opportunities.

04

Organizational Fundraising Capacity

Sometimes the fundraising problem is actually a systems, leadership or structure problem. We help strengthen the infrastructure behind sustainable fundraising.

Board fundraising engagement
Donor stewardship systems
Communications and fundraising alignment
Team structure and roles
Policies, processes and performance measurement

Best for: Leadership and boards ready to resolve internal systems, CRM, donor stewardship, or team structure bottlenecks.

05

Fractional Fundraising Leadership & Advisory

Need senior fundraising expertise without hiring a full-time executive? True Impact can work alongside your CEO, Executive Director, board or fundraising team on a project, short-term or ongoing advisory basis.

Ongoing strategic guidance
Executive and board advisory
Fundraising coaching and mentorship
Campaign and initiative leadership

Best for: Growing organizations that need senior-level fundraising strategy and executive guidance without hiring a full-time director.

You keep ownership of your fundraising. We help you build the strategy and capacity to do it well.
