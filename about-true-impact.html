About True Impact

Led by experience. Built around your organization.

True Impact Philanthropy helps charities, nonprofits and mission-driven organizations build stronger fundraising strategies, meaningful partnerships and the organizational capacity required for sustainable growth.

True Impact was founded by Marsha Clyne, a fundraising strategist and nonprofit leader with nearly 15 years of experience across fundraising, philanthropy, partnerships, communications and organizational development.

Marsha leads client strategy and engagements and, when a project requires additional expertise, brings in trusted professionals with complementary skills. This flexible model allows each engagement to be built around the work that actually needs to be done rather than a predetermined consulting structure.

Meet Our Founder

Marsha Clyne

Fundraising Strategist • Partnership Builder • Nonprofit Leader

For nearly 15 years, I’ve worked in and alongside charitable, nonprofit and community organizations, helping turn missions into strategies, relationships into partnerships and fundraising opportunities into sustainable systems.

My experience spans fundraising strategy, corporate partnerships, grants, major gifts, sponsorship, donor stewardship, community fundraising, organizational development, board engagement, communications, campaign strategy and fundraising operations.

Experience has taught me that fundraising problems rarely exist in isolation. Sometimes what looks like a fundraising problem is actually a communications problem. Sometimes it is governance, donor stewardship, organizational positioning or a lack of the relationships and systems needed for people to invest in the work.

That is why my work looks beyond the ask itself.

Education, Experience & Perspective

Academic knowledge. Hands-on experience. Lived experience.

I hold a Master’s in Philanthropy and Nonprofit Leadership from Carleton University, an Honours degree in Peace and Conflict Studies from the University of Ottawa, and an Associate Degree in Social Communications.

My studies included academic experience through Saint Paul University, federated with the University of Ottawa, with the University of Ottawa reflected on my degree credentials.

My work combines academic understanding with nearly 15 years of hands-on experience working directly with donors, executives, boards, volunteers, communities, corporations, funders and institutional partners, alongside the lived experience that shapes how I understand philanthropy, community, access and impact.

For me, philanthropy is fundamentally about people, relationships, dignity, generosity and creating the conditions for meaningful change.

The True Impact Model

The right expertise for the work.

True Impact operates through a flexible consulting model. Marsha leads strategy and client relationships while additional specialists may be brought into an engagement when their expertise can strengthen the work.

Depending on the project, that may include communications, research, digital strategy, design, technology, grant development, project management or community engagement.

You are not paying for unnecessary overhead. You are investing in the expertise the work requires.

Why True Impact?

We don’t measure philanthropy only by what is given. We measure it by what giving makes possible.

Because fundraising doesn’t begin with money. It begins with purpose. It grows through relationships. And when supported by the right strategy, it becomes the fuel that allows missions to grow, communities to strengthen and meaningful change to endure.

That is True Impact.

We help organizations look beyond the next dollar and build the relationships, strategies and systems that turn generosity into lasting change.
