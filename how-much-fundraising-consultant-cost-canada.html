Fundraising Strategy

How much does a fundraising consultant cost in Canada?

There is no single correct fee because the scope can range from a focused strategy session to months of fractional leadership. The better question is what level of problem you are asking the consultant to solve.

A $750 strategy session and a $7,500 organizational engagement are not the same product. One helps leadership think through a defined issue; the other may involve discovery, analysis, strategy development, systems, stakeholder input and an implementation roadmap. Fractional leadership is different again because you are buying ongoing senior judgment and accountability.

What nonprofit leaders should consider

When comparing proposals, look beyond hourly rates. Ask what decisions will be made, what deliverables will remain with your organization, how much leadership access is included, and whether the work builds internal capacity. Low-cost support can become expensive if it produces more activity without a stronger system.

Putting it into practice

At True Impact, focused strategy sessions start at $750, the Fundraising Health Check starts at $1,500, the 90-Day Fundraising Strategy starts at $3,500, deeper Strategy & Capacity engagements start at $7,500, and fractional fundraising leadership starts at $3,000 per month. Final pricing depends on scope.
