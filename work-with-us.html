A note about how I work

Strategy, not commission fundraising.

True Impact Philanthropy is a strategic fundraising consultancy. My role is to help organizations build stronger fundraising strategies, relationships, systems and internal capacity.

I do not work solely on commission or guarantee specific fundraising results. Successful philanthropy depends on leadership, relationships, organizational reputation, donor readiness, market conditions and consistent implementation.

What I bring is the strategy, experience and guidance to help your organization make better fundraising decisions and build something sustainable.
